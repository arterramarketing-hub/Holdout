# Holdout

A single-file browser shooter: you command a squad of eight, and when a soldier falls you take over
the next one. Modern infantry, N64-style rendering, fought street by street through a mountain town.

Play it by opening `index.html` in a browser (it needs an internet connection — three.js loads from a CDN),
or serve the folder and visit it over http.

- Third person by default, with auto-fire; a first-person mode with manual fire lives in SETTINGS (or press V).
- Desktop: WASD move, Space dive to cover, R reload, Tab lock-on, Q/E camera, 1/2/3 supports, M mute, O settings.
- Phone: left thumb moves, buttons on the right dive, lock, fire and aim.

Everything — models, textures, animation and sound — is generated in code; there are no asset files.
